# Welcome to Discipleship.Earth

**Rooted in the Word. Learning to follow Jesus Christ.**

[Visit Discipleship.Earth](https://discipleship.earth) · [Explore the Twelve](profiles/README.md) · [Follow our progress](docs/development-log.md)

## Why this project exists

Discipleship.Earth is being developed to help people explore the lives of biblical individuals, consider the words of Scripture, and reflect on what it means to follow Jesus Christ.

We begin with the original Twelve disciples. Through their lives, readers encounter questions of faith, obedience, doubt, failure, and responsibility. The aim is to encourage careful reading and personal reflection, with the King James Bible as the foundation for the work.

## What we are building

The project brings together biblical character profiles, Scripture references, sectioned narratives, and visual storytelling. Each developing profile is intended to give readers a clear path through the individual's story, with links and supporting material that invite further study.

The Twelve account profiles have been created. Their biographies and galleries are being developed progressively. Features such as demographic cards, narrative sections, and additional images remain work in progress; a listed account does not mean its complete profile has been published.

Start with the [profile index](profiles/README.md) to find the individuals and their live pages.

## Scripture and storytelling

We use the **King James Bible** for Scripture quotations and references. Our purpose is to help readers engage with the biblical text and examine the passages behind a profile.

Editorial narratives are human-written or AI-assisted interpretations that require review. They should make their supporting references clear and distinguish biblical statements from inference or imaginative reconstruction. A narrator's wording, a reconstructed scene, or a character's imagined thoughts must never be presented as a quotation from Scripture.

Our [proposed editorial standards](docs/editorial-standards.md) explain the intended approach to sourcing, uncertainty, review, and corrections. They remain a proposal and do not certify that every live page has completed that review.

## About the images

Portraits and gallery scenes are modern artistic interpretations, including AI-generated imagery. They are not authenticated likenesses of biblical individuals or photographs of biblical events.

As visual records are published here, we intend to document the prompts, reference choices, significant revisions, and human decisions behind them. Physical appearance, clothing, setting, and estimated age selected for a character model should be understood as artistic choices unless separately supported by an identified source.

## Why the work is public

Readers should be able to inspect the material behind the experience. This repository is where we will publish content records, sources, image-development notes, and meaningful revisions as they become ready for public review.

Some materials are still being prepared. The website may contain newer content than the records available here. The [development log](docs/development-log.md) identifies milestones and planned work so readers can see what has actually been completed.

## Read, examine, and follow along

Visit the profiles, read the referenced passages, and compare the storytelling with Scripture. If you find an error or unclear attribution, you can raise it through this repository's [Issues page](https://github.com/Discipleship-Earth/discipleship-earth/issues). Include the page or file, the statement in question, and the supporting reference where possible.
